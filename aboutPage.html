Sustainability Practices

Best time: Water in the morning so the soil and any splashed leaves can dry during the day.

Check the soil: Stick your finger 1 to 2 inches into the dirt; only water if the soil feels dry. Lighter-colored soil also signals dryness

Water deeply: Pour water slowly at the base until it runs out of the bottom drainage holes, ensuring the whole root ball gets wet

Avoid frequent splashes: Giving plants a thorough soaking once or twice a week is much better than giving them small splashes every day
