Watering Guidelines Section

Watering Guidelines

Best time: Water in the morning so the soil and any splashed leaves can dry during the day.

Check the soil: Stick your finger 1 to 2 inches into the dirt; only water if the soil feels dry. Lighter-colored soil also signals dryness

Water deeply: Pour water slowly at the base until it runs out of the bottom drainage holes, ensuring the whole root ball gets wet

Avoid frequent splashes: Giving plants a thorough soaking once or twice a week is much better than giving them small splashes every day

Sunlight & Soil Requirements

Sunlight Requirements

Full sun: At least 6 to 8 hours of direct sunlight each day. Most vegetables, herbs like basil, and flowers like sunflowers need full sun

Partial sun / Partial shade: 3 to 6 hours of direct sunlight daily. Morning sun is cooler and gentler, while afternoon sun is hotter.

Full shade: Less than 3 hours of direct sunlight per day. Plants like ferns and hostas prefer this cooler, filtered light

Sunlight Requirements

Soil texture: Soil can be sandy, clay, or loamy (a balanced mix). Loamy soil is best for most plants because it drains well while holding moisture

pH levels: Most vegetables and common plants prefer a slightly acidic pH between 6.0 and 7.0.

Nutrients and care: Mix in organic matter like compost to feed the soil and improve drainage. Use slow-release fertilizers to give plants steady nutrition over time

Pest Control Tips

Watering Guidelines

Build healthy soil: Feed your soil with compost and organic fertilizer to grow strong plants that fight off pests naturally.

Practice garden hygiene: Clear away dead leaves, weeds, and fallen fruit so bugs have nowhere to hide or breed.

Water correctly: Give plants the right amount of water to avoid extra moisture that attracts pests.
